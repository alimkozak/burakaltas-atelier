Everything brides ask us

How do I measure myself?

Fill in the detailed measurement card on our online fitting page with a friend and a soft tape measure — or book a measurement session and we take every measurement with you on video. We check all the numbers together before cutting begins.

What if the dress doesn't fit?

Because your gown is cut to your own measurements, big changes are rare. Small tweaks (hem, straps) can be done by any local tailor; if something is off because of our work, contact us and we'll make it right.

How long does it take? Can you rush?

Bridal gowns take 8–12 weeks to make, after-party dresses 4–6 weeks, plus shipping time. Ideally order 4–6 months before your wedding; the “Do I have time?” calculator shows the latest order date for yours. Express production (about 5 weeks) is often possible for a surcharge — ask us with your date.

Can I change the design?

Yes. Neckline, sleeves, train length, colour, lining and embellishment can all be adjusted, and we can design a completely new gown from your idea. We'll confirm any extra cost before you order.

Can I see the fabric before ordering?

We can show every fabric live on a video call, and we can post physical swatches on request.

Will I pay customs or import duties?

This depends on your country and is paid by the buyer on delivery. EU: thanks to the EU–Türkiye customs union usually only import VAT applies. UK: 20% import VAT plus the courier's handling fee; customs duty on goods of Turkish origin is often 0%. USA: since August 2025 all parcels are subject to duties. UAE and the Gulf: usually 5% customs duty plus local VAT (UAE 5%, Saudi Arabia 15%). Write to us for an estimated total for your country.

Are the photos real?

Our gowns are made in our own İzmir atelier, and you'll receive real photos and videos of your gown at every production stage. Follow @burakaltas_atelier to see the workroom and our real brides.

How do I pay — and is it safe?

Orders and payment go through our online stores (Etsy, Trendyol and others), so your payment is handled by the store and covered by its own buyer protection.

Can I return a made-to-measure gown?

Returns and cancellations follow the terms of the store you ordered from, shown on that store's page. Because every gown is made for one person, we check your measurements and design with you, and send progress photos before anything is finished.

Do you make plus-size, petite and modest gowns?

Always. Every gown is drafted for your body, so size is never a limit. Sleeves, higher necklines, linings and longer hems are available on any design.

What arrives in the box?

Your gown, steamed and folded in tissue inside a breathable garment bag, with care instructions. Matching veils and after-party dresses can be shipped together.
