Privacy (KVKK & GDPR)

Who we are

Burak Altaş Atelier is responsible for the personal data described here. You can reach us at the address and email at the bottom of this page.

What we collect — and what we don't

You don't need an account to use this website. When you fill in the enquiry form, book a video appointment or use the measurement card, what you type (for example your name, contact details, wedding date and measurements) is saved only in your own browser, so you don't have to type it again. It reaches us only when you choose to send it — by WhatsApp, email or live chat.

How we use your details

We use what you send us only to answer you, to design and make your gown, to schedule your appointments and to deliver your order. Your measurements and photos are used only for your gown. We never sell your details or share them for advertising.

Services we rely on

  • Etsy — orders and payments are placed through our Etsy shop; Etsy's privacy policy applies to them.
  • WhatsApp, email and live chat — when you write to us, your message travels through these services and their own privacy policies apply. Live chat loads only if you open it.
  • Website delivery — our hosting and content delivery network. Like any website, it receives technical data such as your IP address when it delivers files to your browser. Our typefaces are served from our own server, and Google Maps on the contact page loads only if you press "Show the map".
  • Visitor statistics — if enabled, we use cookie-free, anonymous statistics to see which pages are useful. No profile of you is built.

Cookies and local storage

This website sets no tracking cookies. It uses your browser's local storage only for your convenience: your language, your shortlist, and the details you typed into forms. You can delete all of it at any time with "Delete my details from this device" at the bottom of every page.

How long we keep your details

We keep your messages, measurements and photos only for as long as we need them for your order and for our legal obligations (for example accounting records), and then delete them. You can ask us to delete them sooner at any time.

Your rights

Under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK, Article 11) and, for brides in the EU and UK, the GDPR, you can ask what data we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, object to its use, and receive a copy. Write to us and we will reply within 30 days.

Orders & payment

All orders are placed and paid through our Etsy shop, so your payment is processed securely by Etsy and covered by Etsy's purchase protection. Prices on this website are starting prices in US dollars; the final price for your gown, including any changes you ask for, is confirmed with you before you order.

Made to measure

Every gown is cut to your own measurements. Before cutting begins we confirm your measurements and design with you, usually on a video call. Bridal gowns take about 8–12 weeks to make and after-party dresses about 4–6 weeks; you receive photos and videos as your gown progresses.

Shipping & duties

Finished gowns are shipped from İzmir by tracked, insured express courier, arriving in about 3–7 days in most countries. Import duties, taxes and courier handling fees depend on your country and are paid by the recipient on delivery. Our FAQ explains what to expect for the EU, the UK, the USA and the Gulf.

Returns & alterations

Because each gown is made for one person only, made-to-measure pieces cannot be returned for a change of mind. If something is wrong because of our work — a fault in the making, or a difference from the measurements and design you approved — contact us as soon as you receive your gown, with photos, and we will make it right. Small adjustments such as hem length can also be done by a local tailor.
