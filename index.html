Remote made-to-measure

From our atelier to your door

You never need to visit İzmir. Every step happens over video, with your approval — and because the gown is cut to your body, it needs far fewer alterations than a boutique dress.

  1. 01

    Choose & personalise

    Pick a gown — or bring your own idea. Change the neckline, add sleeves, shorten the train. We sketch it for you before anything is cut.

    Free design consultation
  2. 02

    Measure with us, on video

    Book a measurement session: we guide you and a helper through every point of our measurement card, live on video. No guesswork.

    ≈ 45 minutes · by appointment
  3. 03

    Made by hand in İzmir

    Your pattern is drafted from scratch. You receive photos and videos at cutting, first assembly and embroidery — and approve each stage.

    8 – 12 weeks
  4. 04

    Delivered to your door

    Steamed, wrapped in a breathable garment bag and boxed, then sent to you through the store you ordered from.
