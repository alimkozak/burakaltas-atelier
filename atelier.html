A workroom in Konak, İzmir

The atelier sits in Konak, İzmir — the city that hosts one of Europe's largest bridal fairs and has dressed brides for generations. Here every gown begins as a pattern drafted for one woman only.

We work slowly on purpose: corsets boned by hand, pearls sewn one at a time, feathers placed until they move just right. Then we send the finished gown to wherever in the world you'll wear it.
