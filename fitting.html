Before your call

Ten minutes of preparation

For the measurement session especially, a little preparation means an exact pattern — and a gown that fits the first time.

    The measurement card

    One card, every number

    This is the exact card we use in the atelier. Tell it which gown you have in mind and it shows only the measurements that gown needs. Fill it in before your call or together with us during it — it saves on this device as you type, and you can print it or send it straight to the atelier.

    You'll need a soft tape measure, a thin ribbon and someone to help. Measure over the bra or shapewear you'll wear on the day.

    Video appointments

    Good to know

    Ask something else
    Which app do I need?

    None. The meeting link we send you opens the video room right in your browser, on a phone or a computer — or we simply call you on WhatsApp video, whichever you prefer.

    I live in another time zone — which time is my appointment?

    All times on this page are shown in your own time zone, with the atelier's time (İzmir, GMT+3) next to them. When we confirm, we always write both times.

    Can my mother or my friends join?

    Of course. Share the meeting link with them — they can join from anywhere, even from another country.

    What if I need to change the time?

    Simply write to us on WhatsApp and we'll find a new time together — no fee, no fuss.

    Who takes my measurements?

    A member of our atelier team guides you through every measurement on video. When you book, you can choose whether you'd like a woman or a man — or leave it to us.

    Does the consultation cost anything?

    The design consultation is free and without obligation. Measurement and fitting appointments are part of your order.
